PlywoodEmpire Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Overview

PlywoodEmpire does not have user accounts, so there is no account to delete. This page explains how to delete all data related to the game and how to send us a deletion request.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Open the game, tap the gear button, then Data Deletion, then Delete local data (tap twice to confirm). This erases your progress and settings. Alternatively uninstall the game, which removes all local data.

2. Data held by advertising partners

Ads are provided by Unity Ads. They may hold advertising data linked to your Android Advertising ID. To limit or delete it: (a) in the game open Settings and turn Personal Ads OFF; (b) on Android go to Settings, Privacy, Ads and choose Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID); (c) contact Unity through https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy to use their privacy request options.

3. Email us

Send an email to fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com with the subject PlywoodEmpire data deletion request. Tell us the device model and, if you know it, your Advertising ID. We will reply within 30 days, confirm what data we could find (normally none, as we keep no personal data on our servers) and forward or explain any request that must be sent to a partner.

4. What we keep

We do not keep personal data. We may keep anonymous crash or store statistics provided by Google Play, which are not linked to you by us.
