PlywoodEmpire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

PlywoodEmpire is a free mobile game published by FCP Developer Studio. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. Contact: fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com.

2. Short summary

PlywoodEmpire has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your game progress is stored only on your own device. The game shows ads provided by Unity Ads, which may collect limited device information as described below.

3. Information stored on your device

Game progress (money, buildings, workers, levels) and your settings (volume, shadows, ad choice) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the game or use Settings, then Data Deletion, then Delete local data.

4. Advertising and third parties

The game uses Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. When ads are shown, Unity and its partners may collect and process: your Android Advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app version, language, network type, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about ad impressions and interactions. This is used to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise ads.

We do not control how these companies use data. Please read Unity's privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Your ad choices

On first launch the game asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings, Personal Ads. If you choose NO THANKS, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

6. Children

PlywoodEmpire is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, email fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com and we will help.

7. Retention and security

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data processed by Unity is kept according to Unity's policies. Game data on your device is protected by your device's security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example EU/UK GDPR or California CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to personalised advertising. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are answered by explaining where the data sits. Email fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Data deletion

See Settings, Data Deletion in the game, or our Data Deletion Policy. You can also email fcpdeveloperstudio@gmail.com with the subject: PlywoodEmpire data deletion request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date below changes when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the new version.
